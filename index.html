About this solver

Problem. Each solution opens a set of depots, uses vehicles of limited capacity and visits every customer exactly once. The cost is depot opening costs + fixed vehicle costs + routing costs. A route starts and ends at the same depot, its load may not exceed the vehicle capacity, and the load of all routes of a depot may not exceed the depot capacity. Read the model on the Learn page.

Gap. The gap is gap = 100 × z − zBKSzBKS %, where z is the cost of the solution and zBKS is the best known solution value published for the instance in the literature (see the note on BKS values). A negative gap means the run found a cheaper solution than the published one.

How it runs. The engine is compiled to WebAssembly and runs inside your browser, one Web Worker per seed slot. Your instances and settings are never sent to a server; the page only reports two anonymous counts (page opened, run started) to GoatCounter, an analytics service that does not track personal data. Runs with the same seed, settings and time-independent budget give the same result on any computer; with a time limit the number of iterations done depends on the speed of your device, so costs can differ slightly between devices.

Estimated time. number of seeds ÷ parallel workers (rounded up) × (time limit + about 1 s start-up). It is accurate to roughly ±50% if your computer is busy with other work. Browsers may slow down background tabs: keep this tab in front during a run.
